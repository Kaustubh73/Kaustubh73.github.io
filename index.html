About

I am a Data Scientist at Oracle working on large language models. My research interests lie at the intersection of mechanistic interpretability, multilingual NLP, and AI safety. I am particularly interested in understanding how internal model representations govern multilingual reasoning and safety behavior.

Alongside my job, I am a Research Fellow in the Sentient Futures Project Incubator (John Lund, AE Studio) and the Better Futures Fellowship (AI Safety Collab), and I work on a multilingual refusal benchmark with Prof. Usman Naseem (Macquarie University).

I completed my B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence at IIT Hyderabad in 2025, graduating with the highest CGPA in my department (9.34).

Current Research

Robustness of Moral Consideration Under Adversarial Pressure

Workstream B lead in the Sentient Futures Project Incubator. Tests whether steering along persona vectors can protect moral consideration in LLMs under adversarial fine-tuning. Built the steering and LoRA-erosion pipeline on Qwen3-8B.

Mentors: John Lund (AE Studio / AIAF), Jasmine Brazilek & Maheep Chaudhary (CaML)

Agency-Preserving Alignment & Multiturn Delegation Drift

Studies delegation drift, where models take over decisions from users across multi-turn conversations. Evaluated 5 open-weight models over 720 trajectories (2,880 turns) and found engagement-oriented prompts cause a +23–25% decision-takeover cliff versus autonomy prompts.

Fellowship: Better Futures Fellowship (AI Safety Collab / Forethought)

Multi-Task Multilingual LLM Refusal

Tests whether task wrappers shift LLM refusal across languages. Built a 211k-row benchmark across 15 languages and 8 task wrappers and is evaluating task-conditioned refusal shifts.

Collaborator: Prof. Usman Naseem (Macquarie University)

Projects

Research

  • Multi-Task Multilingual LLM Refusal Evaluation
    With Prof. Usman Naseem (Macquarie University). Tests whether task wrappers shift LLM refusal across languages. Built a 211k-row benchmark (1,761 seeds × 15 languages × 8 task wrappers) extending Sakonii task-over-refusal, replicated the baseline on Llama-3.1-8B with a calibrated OR-Bench-style judge, and is now evaluating task-conditioned refusal shifts.
    Python • PyTorch • HuggingFace • OR-Bench
    [GitHub]
  • PrefKit — Multi-Method Preference Elicitation Toolkit
    Tests whether stated model preferences depend on how they are elicited. Built a toolkit with four elicitation methods (pairwise, Likert, action-choice, best-worst scaling) and a Cross-Method Score, and ran it across Qwen3 (1.7B–14B) on a 24-outcome set for the Apart Digital Minds Sprint (Track 4).
    Python • PyTorch • HuggingFace
    [GitHub]
  • Detection Is Not Containment: Timely Monitoring Determines Whether AI Agent Failures Can Be Prevented
    Team KIG with Gagana M D and María Inés Berdiñas, for the Apart Research × CeSIA AI Incident Response Sprint. Asks whether monitoring can stop an agent failure in time. Built a public-source incident claim-extraction pipeline and a monitoring-intervention experiment showing that detecting an agent action only helps if a last-safe point to stop it remains, including the cost of false positives.
    Python • JSONL • Incident Forensics • Evaluation Protocol
    [Apart Sprint] [GitHub] [Report PDF]
